# NeuroAtlas brand assets

Created with the built-in image generation tool.

## Logo

Final asset: `public/brand/neuroatlas-logo.png`.

Direction: a brain silhouette with blue and sage hemispheres, neural terminals and a gold central junction, with the NEUROATLAS wordmark on navy. The final cleanup prompt preserved the silhouette and wordmark, replaced transparency with navy #0b1326, and requested removal of all speckling and edge artifacts.

## Instagram post

Portrait 4:5 promotional design, using the existing logo, a matte ivory illustrative brain, blue/sage pathways, and gold junctions. Educational positioning, no invented URL or QR code. Main headline updated at the user's request to English: “Explore the Brain”. Feature descriptions remain Arabic.
